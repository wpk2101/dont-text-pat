How prices work

Keeper price = last year's price + $5 × years kept (counting this year)

Last year's price is the higher of his 2025 draft price or the free-agent bid paid for him. A $20 player climbs like this:

$251st year
$352nd year
$503rd year
$704th year
  • Only players on a roster at the end of last season can be kept.
  • Pickups made after Feb 23, 2026 can't be kept this year.
  • A player's years-kept count follows him through trades and drops.
  • For next year: players must be picked up by Feb 22, 2027 to be keepable.
